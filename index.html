Safety & Transition Matrices

Action Delegation Profiles

Higher-risk actions must strictly deny AI execution roles. The agent is a "preparer," shifting humans to "approvers."

Risk Tier Delegation Logic
  • Tier 1 (Read-Only): Fully Automated (Log scanning, audit parsing).
  • Tier 2 (Low-Impact): Automated with async review (Jira updates).
  • Tier 3 (High-Impact): 1-Click Human Approval required (IP block).
  • Tier 4 (Critical): Multi-factor Officer Auth required (Server isolation).
