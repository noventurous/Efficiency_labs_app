Framework 01 · AI-Driven Business Transformation

From "we use ChatGPT sometimes" to a governed AI program.

Original synthesis inspired by widely-cited transformation research.

1 · North Star — vision before tooling
  • Clear, articulated vision — one sentence everyone can repeat.
  • Measurable goals — incremental or transformational, picked deliberately.
  • Strategy alignment — AI enables the business, not the other way around.
PMO tip: if you can't write the vision in one sentence, you don't have one yet.
2 · Customer and market signals
  • Market landscape — competitor bets, new entrants.
  • Customer needs — explicit feedback and latent behavior.
PMO tip: AI roadmaps should be driven by customer pain first, technology capability second.
3 · Operating system — who decides what runs
  • Central team — exec sponsor + governance + security + ethics.
  • Rapid-response teams — flat, cross-functional, agile.
The10/20/70 rule: 10% algorithms · 20% tech · 70% people and process.
4 · AI champions — volunteer-driven ownership
  1. Identify — intrinsically motivated employees.
  2. Recruit — sell the inspiring vision, not the tool.
  3. Motivate — flat-structure work and direct access to leaders.
  4. Empower — place them in influential positions during rollout.
5 · Personal transformation — make it human

People see transformation as a threat, burden, or opportunity.

  1. Help them define their aspiration.
  2. Help them map strengths and gaps; design upskilling.
  3. Help them build a personal plan they share publicly.
PMO tip: lead the personal-transformation work first.

Framework 03 · Prompt Engineering for PMs

Formulas and patterns that survive production.

Original synthesis of widely-used prompt patterns. Click any starter to copy it.

Role · Task · Format
Char · Req · Ex · Adjust · Type · Eval
Spec · Meas · Ach · Rel · Time
Sit · Task · Action · Result
Sit · Chal · Act · Res · Fee

When to use which formula

  • RTF — simple, well-defined asks.
  • CREATE — multi-step or nuanced tasks.
  • SMART — when output must be measurable.
  • STAR — behavioral or scenario questions.
  • SCARF — when team dynamics matter.

Advanced patterns

  • Chain-of-thought — break complex tasks into steps.
  • Persona — steer to a specific expertise.
  • Flipped interaction — let the LLM ask you questions.
  • Self-consistency — validate across iterations.
  • Scenario exploration — simulate outcomes.

Ethics & safety checklist

  • Validation — request citations & spot-check.
  • PII — never share personal data with hosted LLMs.
  • Bias — diverse test prompts.
  • Transparency — document decisions.

Track every prompt you ship

  • Task · prompt text · formula used
  • LLM + version
  • Response example
  • Metrics: accuracy, relevance, conciseness

Framework 04 · Organizational Support for GenAI

Why most AI projects fail — and the practitioner playbook that fixes it.

Built from Efficiency Labs' deployment experience.

Project governance

  • Steering committee — exec + ethics + security + PMO.
  • Decision rights: who approves, halts, audits.
  • Risk register tied to outputs, not just delivery.

Talent & culture

  • AI champions network — top-down sponsor + bottom-up volunteers.
  • Skills inventory: prompt literacy, evaluation, governance.
  • Career path: prompt engineer → AI PM → portfolio lead.

Ethics & trust

  • Bias testing on every new workflow before production.
  • Transparency log of what the AI decided and why.
  • Human-in-the-loop for every high-risk action.

Five-step support model

2 · Operating systemCT + RRTs + governance.
3 · ChampionsIdentify → Recruit → Motivate → Empower.
