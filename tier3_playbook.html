Asset 01

The Task Viability Scoring Matrix (MAUT Model)

💡 Practitioner Tip: How to Run a 45-Minute Workflow Audit Sprint

Interview functional leads (Engineering, Support, PMO, Finance) and list their top 5 time-consuming weekly tasks. Ask three questions to assign scores: (1) "If this task fails, what is the dollar or security cost?" ($R$), (2) "How many times per week does a human touch this?" ($V$), and (3) "Is the input a structured form/webhook or freeform text?" ($D$).

Evaluation Vector Score 1 – 3 (Low) Score 4 – 7 (Moderate) Score 8 – 10 (High)
Risk of Failure (R) Reversible, internal-only. Zero financial loss. Minor friction; resolved within standard SLA. Direct revenue loss, security breach, legal liability.
Volume of Execution (V) < 10 runs / week. Trivial overhead. 50 – 500 runs / week. Measurable drag. > 1,000 runs / week. Primary bottleneck.
Data Determinism (D) Unstructured voice, vague email, handwriting. Semi-structured JSON, support tickets. Deterministic schema, webhooks, strict CSV.
Mathematical Decision Formula
Viability Index (VI) = (Volume × Data Determinism) / Risk of Failure
VI ≥ 15.0 (and R ≤ 3) Fully Autonomous Execution. Deploy zero-touch webhooks with automated telemetry logging.
5.0 ≤ VI < 15.0 (or 4 ≤ R ≤ 7) Human-in-the-Loop. Agent prepares synthesis; human manager authorizes in 1-click.
VI < 5.0 (or R ≥ 8) Strictly Manual. Risk exceeds autonomous safety bounds. Human execution mandatory.
⚡ Advanced Strategy: The "Split-Node" Workaround for High-Risk Tasks

The Problem: A task has massive volume ($V=10$) but high financial risk ($R=9$), yielding a low Viability Index that would normally block automation (e.g., executing $5,000 refund disbursements).
The Solution: Decompose the workflow into two discrete nodes: (1) Node A (Ingestion & Calculation): Low risk ($R=1$) ➔ Fully automated data extraction. (2) Node B (Execution): High risk ($R=9$) ➔ Gated behind a 1-click CFO approval Slack callback. This reclaims 90% of manual effort while preserving 100% human fiduciary control.

Asset 02

SecOps Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Matrix

🛡️ Security Lead Tip: Implementing the 1-Click Approval Channel

Create a private Slack channel named #ai-approvals-leadership. When a Tier 3 action occurs (e.g., blocking an external IP or approving a contract redline), Make.com sends an interactive Block Kit card displaying: (1) The incident summary, (2) Grounded evidence, and (3) Two buttons: [Approve] and [Reject]. The webhook halts until an authorized manager clicks the button.
