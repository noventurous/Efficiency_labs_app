Asset 01

The Task Viability Scoring Matrix

Vector Score 1 – 3 (Low) Score 4 – 7 (Moderate) Score 8 – 10 (High)
Risk of Failure (R) Reversible, internal-only. Zero financial loss. Minor friction; resolved within standard SLA. Direct revenue loss, security breach, legal liability.
Volume of Execution (V) < 10 runs / week. Trivial overhead. 50 – 500 runs / week. Measurable drag. > 1,000 runs / week. Primary bottleneck.
Data Structure (D) Unstructured voice, vague email, handwriting. Semi-structured JSON, support tickets. Deterministic schema, webhooks, strict CSV.
Mathematical Decision Formula
Viability Index (VI) = (Volume × Data Structure) / Risk of Failure
VI ≥ 15.0 (and R ≤ 3) Fully Autonomous Execution. Deploy zero-touch webhooks.
5.0 ≤ VI < 15.0 (or 4 ≤ R ≤ 7) Human-in-the-Loop. Agent prepares; human approves in 1-click.
VI < 5.0 (or R ≥ 8) Strictly Manual. Risk exceeds autonomous safety bounds.
